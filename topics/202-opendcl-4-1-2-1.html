You may have to uninstall your old 4.0.1.1, before 4.1.2.1 will install. Somewhere along the road the installer was updated to do the uninstall for you then install the new version, but I think your version is still to old.
In the editor if you go to Help-->about it will tell you what version it is.
Also the arx's will now be located in the "C:\Program Files\Common Files\OpenDCL" folder not the "C:\Program Files\OpenDCL" folder.
Hope this help.
