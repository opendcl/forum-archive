owenwengerd 2009-03-09 01:50 UTC
No, the list will not be evaluated. The expression '(1 2 3) is just an abbreviated form of (quote (1 2 3)). The (quote) function does not evaluate its argument. Therefore:
(setq N 3)
'(1 N) == (quote (1 N)) => (1 N)
(list (1 N)) => (1 3)
