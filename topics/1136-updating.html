Hi velasquez, first of all, 6.0.0.19 is an alpha release. so be carefully in using in a production environment (for me a production environment is the developer's machine and his source files). 95 % of all forms that worked properly in 5.1.2.3 are also running perfectly in 6.0.0.19.
