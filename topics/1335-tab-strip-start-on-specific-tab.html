Fred
bazzacad 2010-09-01 17:18 UTC
ya like Fred said, do something like this...


(defun c:Untitled_Form1_OnInitialize (/)
  (dcl_TabStrip_SetCurSel Untitled_Form1_TabStrip1 2);; <-- load the 3rd tab page
