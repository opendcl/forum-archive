owenwengerd 2015-02-25 15:45 UTC
There is no way to have both installed at once, although I suppose you could keep the OpenDCL 7 ARX modules in another folder and load them manually. Your OpenDCL 7 project will work in OpenDCL 8, so you can just update to 8 and leave your old projects unchanged.
Jim 2015-02-27 23:50 UTC
You mean I can update to 8 and still develop for my Acad 2004 version. What about underscores and such?
owenwengerd 2015-02-28 02:13 UTC
IYour old project will work fine in OpenDCL 8, it's just that if you start working on it in 8 then you can't later move it back to 7 again. However, if you just want to test in Bricscad 15, you can do that without changing anything in 8, then go back to active development in 7 once you're finished testing. I hope that makes sense.
