Hi, the same event will be thrown when you press esc

(defun c:Metre_Metre_OnCancelClose (intIsESC)     
  (cond
    ((= intIsESC 0) (princ "\nEnter was pressed!\n"))
    ((= intIsESC 1) (princ "\nESC was pressed!\n"))
  ); cond
); c:Metre_Metre_OnCancelClose

Regards, Fred
