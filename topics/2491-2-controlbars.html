owenwengerd 2017-03-18 21:14 UTC
Control position is a combination of position and size of the newly placed control in relation to what is already on the screen at that point. If you want a new control bar to be on the right of an existing control bar, you could try to make its position equal to the upper right corner of the existing bar.
