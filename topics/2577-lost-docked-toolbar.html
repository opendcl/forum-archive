Jim 2018-02-25 00:13 UTC
The end of a exported profile.arg was corrupted. That is where docked control bar positions where stored back when menus were .mns files..
When cui files came to be they were used for storing control bar positions.

In .arg, Delete from  "__BEGIN_PROFILE_STORAGE_R16__" to the end of the file to clear corruption.
