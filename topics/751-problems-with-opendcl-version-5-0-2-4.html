fred_tomke 2009-05-11 15:32 UTC
To close the form, close it by Window > Close or by the close button in the upper right just below the red-cross-application-close-button (however it is called correctly  :) ). Since you have maximized the client window (the container for your form), you will find it at the upper right as in every Windows product.

Fred
